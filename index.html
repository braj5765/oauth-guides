About & limits

What is checked

Every guide is built from the provider's official documentation. Each claim carries a confidence label and a source link, and each guide shows the date it was last checked.

What is not

Nothing here was tried hands-on. No real OAuth app was created and no review was submitted. Approval times, console layouts and review rules change without notice. Where a provider publishes no figure, the guide says so and gives a labelled estimate.

Generated guides

For apps outside the library, a guide is generated by a model from public documentation. Treat it as a starting point and check the linked sources before you rely on it.
